❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about Aurora Travels

How do I make a booking with Aurora Travels? +

You can book through our online booking form, call our hotline at +63 2 800 1234, or visit any of our branch offices. Our consultants will confirm your booking within 24 hours and guide you through every step of the process.

What is included in the travel packages? +

All Aurora Travels packages include return flights, hotel accommodation (minimum 4-star), airport transfers, local transportation, professional tour guides, travel insurance, and pre-booked attraction tickets. Specific inclusions vary per package — check each package's details for a full list.

Can I customize my itinerary? +

Absolutely! We specialize in fully customized itineraries. Tell us your dream destination, travel style, budget, and dates — our consultants will craft a personalized journey that perfectly matches your preferences. Custom packages are available for solo travelers, couples, families, and groups.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX), online bank transfers, GCash, PayMaya, and direct bank deposits. A 25% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm the booking, with the full balance due 45 days before departure.

Do you provide visa assistance? +

Yes! Visa assistance is included in all our packages. Our visa specialists will guide you through the complete application process, prepare your documents, and provide official sponsorship letters where required. Processing fees (charged by the embassy) are the client's responsibility.

What is your cancellation policy? +

We offer free cancellation within 48 hours of booking. Cancellations 30–44 days before departure receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 29 days of departure are non-refundable. We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance (included in our packages) for full protection.

Is travel insurance included? +

Yes, comprehensive travel insurance is included in all Aurora Travels packages. Coverage includes medical emergencies (up to USD 100,000), trip cancellation, lost or delayed baggage, flight delays, and emergency evacuation. Additional top-up coverage is available on request.

Are group discounts available? +

Groups of 5–9 guests receive a 10% discount on total package price. Groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount plus a complimentary group coordinator service throughout the trip. Contact our group travel team for customized group packages and pricing.

Do you organize corporate travel? +

Yes! Aurora Travels has a dedicated Corporate Travel Division handling incentive trips, team-building retreats, corporate events, and executive travel management. We work with over 150 corporate clients across the Philippines. Email corporate@auroratravels.ph for details.

How do I reach you in an emergency during my trip? +

Our 24/7 emergency travel support line is always available at +63 917 123 4567 (WhatsApp/Viber). Every client also receives a personal emergency contact card with their local destination representative's number before departure. We never leave you without support.
