Our Story

Born from a Passion for Travel

Aurora Travels was founded in 2012 by travel enthusiasts who believed that every person deserves to experience the world at its most magnificent. What began as a small boutique travel consultancy in Makati City has grown into one of the Philippines' most trusted and award-winning luxury travel agencies.

Our name is inspired by the Aurora Borealis—the Northern Lights—one of Earth's most awe-inspiring natural phenomena. Just as the aurora illuminates the sky with extraordinary color and wonder, we illuminate our clients' lives with transformative travel experiences that leave them breathless.

Today, we are a team of over 50 dedicated travel professionals serving clients across Southeast Asia, with partnerships in more than 85 countries worldwide. Our commitment to excellence has never wavered since our very first booking.

About the Developer

Meet the Student Behind Aurora Travels

Hello! I'm Adsmer Baldera, a 15-year-old Grade 10 Osmeña student from Palawan National School (PNS). This website, Aurora Travels, is my ICT project created as part of our Grade 10 HTML lessons under the guidance of Ma'am Grace Parangue.

Developing this website has been one of the most challenging and rewarding experiences of my school life. For almost three months, I dedicated my time to planning the design, writing HTML, improving layouts with CSS, fixing errors, and refining every page until it became the project it is today. Every challenge became another lesson that strengthened my patience, perseverance, and determination.

Throughout our HTML lessons, I learned how to structure web pages using semantic HTML, create responsive layouts with CSS, organize content effectively, and build a professional-looking website from scratch. This experience also taught me creativity, discipline, critical thinking, and the importance of never giving up when facing difficult problems.

Aurora Travels is more than just an ICT project—it represents my growth as a student and the knowledge I gained throughout Grade 10. I sincerely thank Ma'am Grace Parangue for teaching us the fundamentals of web development and inspiring us to continue learning. I hope this project reflects my dedication, hard work, and passion for creating something meaningful.

📅 Our Journey

A Decade of Excellence

From a two-person startup with a big dream to an internationally recognized luxury travel agency — here is how the Aurora Travels story unfolded year by year.

2012 — Foundation

Aurora Travels is Born

Founded by CEO Maria Dela Cruz and COO Jose Reyes in Makati City with a team of 5 travel consultants and a vision to redefine Philippine luxury travel.

2014 — First Award

DOT Excellence Award

Received the Department of Tourism Excellence Award for Outstanding Service, recognizing Aurora Travels as a leader in client satisfaction and tour quality.

2016 — Expansion

Regional Office Opens

Expanded operations with new offices in Cebu City and Davao, growing the team to 25 consultants and serving over 3,000 clients annually.

2018 — International

IATA Accreditation

Achieved full IATA accreditation, enabling direct ticketing partnerships with all major international airlines and significantly expanding our global reach.

2020 — Resilience

Navigating the Pandemic

Despite unprecedented global challenges, Aurora Travels pivoted to virtual travel planning and emerged stronger, launching enhanced digital services and flexible booking policies.

2024 — Present

Industry Leader

Celebrating 12 years and 15,000+ satisfied travelers, Aurora Travels is now the Philippines' premier luxury travel agency with 48 industry awards and a 98% satisfaction rate.
