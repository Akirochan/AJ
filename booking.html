📋 Travel Policies

Our Booking Policies

💳 Payment Policy

A 25% non-refundable deposit secures your booking. The remaining balance is due 45 days before departure. Credit card, bank transfer, and GCash are accepted.

🔄 Cancellation Policy

Free cancellation within 48 hours of booking. 50% refund for cancellations 30–44 days before departure. No refund within 29 days of departure.

🛡 Travel Insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, lost baggage, and flight delays is included in all packages.

🛂 Visa Assistance

Aurora Travels provides full visa application assistance and documentation support for all destinations. Processing fees may apply depending on the destination.

✈ Flight Changes

Flight rescheduling is subject to airline policies and availability. We recommend booking early to secure preferred flight schedules. Fees may apply.

👥 Group Bookings

Groups of 5 or more guests receive a 10% discount. Groups of 10 or more receive 15% off plus a complimentary group coordinator service.
